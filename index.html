    Help — 3D Viewer

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • Ctrl/Cmd + Z — Undo / Annuler
    • Ctrl/Cmd + Y — Redo / Refaire
    • Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z — Redo

    Mouse & touch controls

    • Left drag — Orbit camera (rotate around target)
    • Right drag — Pan camera (move view)
    • Middle drag / scroll — Zoom
    • Right click (no drag) — Context menu
    • Left click on model — Material name bubble + select material
    • Double-click on mesh — Frame that element
    • Double-click on ground — Ground editor
    • Double-click on a light helper — Open lights panel focused on that light
    • Touch 1 finger — Orbit
    • Touch 2 fingers — Zoom / pan

    Supported formats

    • .glb / .gltf — glTF 2.0 (embedded textures)
    • .fbx — FBX
    • .zip — archive with FBX/glTF + textures
